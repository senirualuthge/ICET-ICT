01 — About

A little about me

I'm a web developer and ICT student who discovered programming through a curiosity about how websites actually work. Today I focus on the front end: semantic markup, responsive layouts, and interactions that feel effortless.

I care about accessibility and performance as much as visual polish — a nice-looking site that keyboard users can't navigate isn't finished. Outside of code you'll find me sketching UI ideas, reading about design systems, or tinkering with side projects.
